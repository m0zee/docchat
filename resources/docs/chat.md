## Asking

Type a question and press Enter (Shift+Enter adds a new line). The answer streams in as it is written.

Every fact in an answer carries a number like [1] that matches a source below it. A source shows the document title, the page and a snippet; click it to read the whole passage.

## When the answer isn't in your documents

DocChat only answers from passages that match your question closely enough. If none do, it replies "I couldn't find that in your documents." This costs no AI call, and it keeps DocChat from inventing an answer.

## Choosing documents

By default a chat searches all your ready documents. Use the **All my documents** menu to tick only some of them. The choice is saved with the conversation.

## Follow-up questions

DocChat reads the last few messages of the conversation, so "and for children?" after a question about fees works as you'd expect. Start a **New chat** when you change topic.

## Who writes the answer

When your browser supports it, an **AI** menu appears next to the document picker:

- **DocChat server** (default): the answer is written with DocChat's or your workspace's AI key.
- **This device**: your browser writes the answer with its own AI model. See [AI in the browser](/docs/device-ai).

Under each answer, a small line shows the time it took and, for server answers, the tokens used. It also says "from cache" when a repeated question was answered from the cache, and "written on this device" for on-device answers.

## Limits

Each person can ask 20 questions a minute. Conversations are private to the person who started them.
