## 1. Create an account

Sign up with your name, email and a password. DocChat creates a workspace for you straight away. A workspace is your organisation's space: its documents, chats, people and settings are kept apart from every other workspace.

## 2. Upload a document

Open **Documents** and drop a PDF, TXT or Markdown file on the page, or pick one. The status badge shows **Processing** while DocChat reads the file, then **Ready**. Most files take a few seconds.

## 3. Ask a question

Open **Chat** and type a question. The answer streams in with numbered citations like [1], and the sources are listed underneath: document, page and a short snippet. Click a source to read the whole passage.

Try questions your document answers, and one it doesn't. For the second, DocChat replies "I couldn't find that in your documents." without calling the AI at all.

## 4. Invite your team

Owners open **Workspace > Members** to add people who already have an account, or to send an invite link by email. See [Workspaces and members](/docs/workspaces).
